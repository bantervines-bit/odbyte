## Bonus: Tips for Better Prompts

Want to get even better results? Follow these proven strategies:

1. **Be Specific** - The more details you provide, the better the output. Include context, audience info, and desired tone.

2. **Set Clear Constraints** - Word count, character limits, or specific requirements help AI deliver exactly what you need.

3. **Define Your Audience** - Always tell ChatGPT who you're targeting. Demographics and psychographics matter.

4. **Request Variations** - Ask for multiple options (5-10) to find the best one through testing.

5. **Include Examples** - Show ChatGPT what "good" looks like by providing reference examples.

6. **Iterate and Refine** - Don't settle for first output. Ask for revisions with specific feedback.
