Marketing is evolving fast. These 5 prompts will help you stay ahead of the competition and create better content in less time.

## 1. Email Subject Line Generator

Email subject lines can make or break your campaign. This prompt creates multiple variations instantly.

**Prompt:**

Write 10 email subject lines for [product/service]. The email is about [main benefit]. Target audience: [describe audience]. Tone: [casual/professional/urgent]. Include emojis where relevant.

**Why it works:** Specificity is key. The more context you provide, the better AI can tailor subject lines that resonate with your audience.

## 2. Social Media Calendar Creator

Stop staring at blank calendars. Get a month of content ideas in seconds.

**Prompt:**

Create a 30-day social media content calendar for [brand/niche]. Platform: [Instagram/LinkedIn/Twitter]. Goal: [awareness/engagement/sales]. Include post ideas, captions, and hashtags.

**Why it works:** AI generates diverse content aligned with your goals and platform requirements, saving hours of brainstorming.

### Tips for Using This Prompt

Customize these elements for best results:
- Specify your niche clearly
- Mention content pillars
- Include brand voice guidelines

## Key Takeaways

Remember these important points:

- Be specific with audience and goals
- Include constraints (word count, tone)
- Iterate on outputs for perfection
- Save prompts that work for you
