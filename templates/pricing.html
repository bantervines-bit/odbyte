Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade to Diamond anytime. If you choose monthly billing, and want to upgrade to yearly you can do after the end of the monthly plan. Annual users get access for the full year.

What happens to my prompts if I downgrade?

Your existing prompts are safe. You just won't be able to create new ones beyond the free limit until next month.
